# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and open to different viewpoints.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, publishing private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate are not welcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Report concerns privately through the repository's security reporting channel.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
